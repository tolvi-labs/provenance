## [21:26] Session — Brainstormed and shelved the engineer-defense "chaos receipt"

**Tickets:** none

### What happened
- Brainstormed a churn-tracking tool born from a common story (engineer blamed for slowness while management churns requirements), considered as Provenance's first humane use case. Took it end to end: engineer-owned/private, passive + opt-in annotation capture, two-tier receipt (hard facts + signed, time-anchored annotations), Provenance-seed home with Bastion feeding reversals.
- Stress-tested it and shelved it. The access model was sound but the framing fails: tools amplify intent rather than install it (useless against a manager who will not look), burden of proof sits on the accused, selection-neutrality is unfixable in a defense artifact, and the mirror lands on the wrong person (the verbal churner never touches the tooling). Any consequential churn metric also Goodharts the decision-capture source it reads from.
- Separated the two problems the story conflates: in-workflow decision churn (tractable, on Tolvi's thesis) vs top-down verbal chaos (an org-power problem software cannot fix). Concluded Provenance's identity stays decision/authorship attestation, consistent with [[2026-07-04-provenance-durability-not-compliance]]; churn is at most a private, non-consequential lens, never a product or KPI.

### Files touched
- `vault/decisions/2026-07-08-chaos-receipt-wrong-use-case-churn-is-a-lens.md` — new decision banking the analysis

### Left open
- If churn is ever surfaced, only as a calibrated, private, non-consequential reversal-cost nudge at plan-time (Bastion/Tolvi), never a metric. Nothing to build now.
